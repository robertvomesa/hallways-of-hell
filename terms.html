What signup does

The form records the email address and/or mobile number you provide only when you separately select consent for that channel. Email consent is not required for text signup, and text consent is not required for email signup. Neither is a condition of purchase. Submitting the form records your contact details and consent in an organizer-managed spreadsheet; the form does not itself send messages.

Email announcements

If you choose email updates, you agree to receive future Hallways of Hell marketing announcements, event date and ticket notices, and promotions by email. Each marketing email will include an unsubscribe method. You can ask the organizer to remove your signup through the official Instagram account listed in the Privacy Policy.

Text message announcements

If you choose text updates, you agree to receive recurring Hallways of Hell marketing text messages, including event date and ticket notices and promotions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Consent is not transferable to another person. After opting out, you will no longer receive marketing texts unless you separately opt in again.

The organizer has not connected a messaging service to the signup form. Texts will be sent only if the organizer later configures a messaging service and imports contacts who selected SMS consent. Hallways of Hell will return in October 2027; exact dates and ticket details have not been announced.

See the Privacy Policy for information about signup data and service providers.
