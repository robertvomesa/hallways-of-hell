Website information

This website is operated for Hallways of Hell, an annual Halloween attraction presented by Victory Outreach Mesa. The signup form is hosted by Google Apps Script and stores accepted entries in a spreadsheet managed by the organizer.

Links to external services, including Instagram and map services, are governed by those services’ own privacy practices.

Email and text signup

You may provide an email address, a mobile number, or both. Each channel has its own unchecked, optional marketing consent box. A contact detail is saved only when its matching consent is selected. The form records the submission time, the consent selection, and the exact consent wording shown. It does not send email or text messages when you submit.

Signup data is stored in an organizer-managed spreadsheet. Google Apps Script and Google Sheets process the information to receive and store the submission. The organizer may export consented contacts to a customer management system for future Hallways of Hell announcements and promotions, using only the channel(s) each person selected. No customer management or messaging service is connected to this form at this time.

Email and mobile information submitted through the form is not sent to Google Analytics. Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Google processes the data to provide the form and spreadsheet service.

When messages are sent in the future, marketing emails will include an unsubscribe method, and text recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can ask the organizer to correct or remove your signup before it is imported by contacting Hallways of Hell through the official Instagram account below.

No previous attendee information is being added without that person’s signup. The organizer will retain signup records in the private sheet until they are removed by the organizer or a removal request is received.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Hallways of Hell through its official Instagram account.
